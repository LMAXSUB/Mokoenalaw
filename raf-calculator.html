Use this only to explore a simple scenario. A proper RAF calculation can require actuarial evidence, contingencies, career assumptions, inflation, tax and other case-specific factors.

What this does

Past loss = monthly income × months without income.

Illustrative future loss = annual income × remaining working years × estimated reduction.

The simple model does not apply actuarial discounting, contingencies, statutory limits, inflation or tax. It should not be presented as an expected settlement or court award.
