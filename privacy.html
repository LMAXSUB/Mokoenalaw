WEBSITE NOTICE

Privacy and responsible use of this website.

This page is a practical website notice. It should be reviewed and adapted to the firm's actual information-handling practices before publication as a formal privacy policy.

Information you submit

If you submit an enquiry, you may provide information such as your name, contact details and a description of your legal issue. Avoid sending passwords, banking credentials or unnecessary sensitive information through a general web form.

How enquiries may be used

Information submitted through the website may be used to respond to your enquiry, arrange a consultation and assess whether the firm can assist. The firm should retain, secure and process personal information in accordance with applicable law and its own documented privacy practices.

Third-party services

This website may use external services such as GitHub Pages, WhatsApp or email links. Those services may have their own terms and privacy practices.

Security

No website or ordinary email channel should be treated as a guarantee of secure transmission. Use a secure document-sharing method where the firm provides one for sensitive documents.

Legal advice

Website content is general information and does not constitute legal advice or create an attorney-client relationship.

Contact

For questions about this website notice, contact Lungelo Mokoena Attorneys using the firm's published contact details.
