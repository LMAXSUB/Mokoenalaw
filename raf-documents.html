RAF claim document checklist

A practical starting checklist for gathering information for a potential RAF matter.

Common documents

Certified identity documents; accident and police material; witness details; hospital and medical records; medical reports; proof of earnings where loss of earnings is claimed; proof of medical expenses; and a Serious Injury Assessment Report where required for general damages.

Check current requirements

The RAF’s published forms and guidance should be checked because document requirements can vary by claim type and circumstances.

Important: This is general information, not legal advice. Laws, procedures and deadlines can change, and the facts of an individual matter must be considered.
