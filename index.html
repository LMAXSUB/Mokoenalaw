About

Lungelo B. Mokoena

Young, shrewd, and ambitious, Mr Mokoena is an admitted legal practitioner who has spent considerable time establishing himself in the legal fraternity — and, in turn, this firm.

  • LLB, University of Johannesburg
  • Law School, University of Pretoria
  • L.E.A.D. — Practice Management Training & Medical Law

Mr Mokoena is a firm believer in not only defending clients, but championing them — treating the practice of law as inseparable from the lives his clients actually lead.

His areas of expertise span medical law, administration of estates and trusts, conveyancing, corporate law, and entertainment law, alongside other subdivisions of civil and criminal law. The firm is based in Brakpan and proudly serves clients across Boksburg, Benoni and the wider Ekurhuleni metro.
