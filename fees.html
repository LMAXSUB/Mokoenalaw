Fees & Billing

A practical overview of LMA's fee structure, fixed-fee services, hourly billing, disbursements and payment terms.

Request a consultation Understand consultation fees
Important

Fees are confirmed before work begins.

Attorney fees in South Africa are generally negotiable. LMA's fee schedule distinguishes between the firm's own commercial rates, applicable guideline tariffs and statutory rules. The applicable arrangement should be confirmed in a written fee agreement or engagement letter before work begins.

RAF & personal injury matters

Contingency fees

No-win, no-fee arrangements

Contingency fee agreements are governed by the Contingency Fees Act 66 of 1997 and must be in writing and in the prescribed form.

Success-fee structure

Where a success fee is used, the firm's fee schedule records the statutory cap as the lesser of double the normal fee or 25% of the capital amount awarded, with the 25% cap treated as VAT-inclusive following the cited Gauteng High Court judgment in De Bod v Road Accident Fund.

Disbursements

Medico-legal, actuarial, counsel, correspondent and other third-party costs are dealt with separately in the contingency agreement.

A contingency matter requires its own written agreement. The terms applicable to a particular matter should be confirmed before work begins.

Disbursements & third-party costs

Billed separately

Disbursements are amounts paid to third parties on a client's behalf and recovered at cost, without a mark-up, according to the LMA fee schedule.

Court & legal costs

Sheriff's fees, counsel's fees and correspondent attorney fees may apply.

Registration & official fees

Deeds Office, CIPC and Master's Office fees may apply depending on the matter.

Experts & administration

Medico-legal, actuarial and expert witness fees, advertising, travel, printing, courier and relevant bank charges may apply.

Where material, disbursements should be estimated and disclosed to the client in advance.
