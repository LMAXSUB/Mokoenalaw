Criminal Law

Arrest and bail: general information

What to prepare when someone has been arrested and needs legal assistance.

Get the basic facts together

Record when and where the arrest occurred, the police station or court involved, the charge if known, any court date or case number, and relevant identification details.

Early legal assistance

Criminal procedure involves formal processes and deadlines. A lawyer can explain the charge, procedural stage and options available in the particular circumstances.

Important: This is general information, not legal advice. Laws, procedures and deadlines can change, and the facts of an individual matter must be considered.
