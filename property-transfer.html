Property transfer and conveyancing: a starting guide

A plain-language overview of some information involved in a South African property transfer.

Common information

Parties’ identification and contact details, property information, the sale agreement, bond and lender information where applicable, and municipal/rates information where relevant.

Why it can take time

Transfers can involve the buyer, seller, conveyancer, lender and relevant authorities. The exact process depends on the transaction.

Important: This is general information, not legal advice. Laws, procedures and deadlines can change, and the facts of an individual matter must be considered.
