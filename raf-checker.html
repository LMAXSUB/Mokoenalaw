Road Accident Fund claim assessment

Answer a few questions to identify issues worth discussing with a legal practitioner. This is an informational screening tool, not a legal opinion or a guarantee that a claim will succeed.

Question 1 of 8

Was there a road accident involving a motor vehicle in South Africa?

Were you injured, or are you claiming because someone died?

What was your role?

Was the other vehicle/driver identified?

Did you receive medical treatment?

Did the accident affect your work, income or earning capacity?

Do you have documents such as hospital records, police/accident reports or proof of income?

Have you already lodged a RAF claim?

Before you begin

Use this tool as a starting point only. Supporting material may include accident reports, medical records, proof of earnings and claim correspondence.

Time limits can depend on the circumstances. Discuss the accident date and all relevant dates with an attorney rather than relying on this tool.
