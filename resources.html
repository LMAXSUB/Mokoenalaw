Frequently asked questions

Start here
How do RAF claims work?

A Road Accident Fund claim depends on the circumstances of the accident, the claimant's injuries and the evidence supporting the various heads of damage. Use the RAF Research Centre for published judgments and awards, then obtain advice on your own circumstances.

What information should I keep after an accident?

Keep accident details, case or police information, medical records, invoices, employment and income information, photographs and witness details where available.

What is a consultation fee?

A consultation fee generally pays for the lawyer's time spent understanding the matter and providing initial legal guidance. It is distinct from a quotation for handling the entire matter.

Can I ask a legal question before booking?

Yes. You can send a general enquiry through the contact form. Do not send confidential or highly sensitive documents until appropriate arrangements have been made.

Fees should be clear.

A consultation, a quotation for a legal service and a contingency-fee arrangement are different things. Ask what the fee covers before instructing an attorney.
